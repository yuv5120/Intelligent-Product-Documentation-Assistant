# Product User Manual

## Getting Started

Welcome to our amazing product! This manual will help you get started quickly.

### What's in the Box

Your product package includes:
- Main device unit
- Power adapter (100-240V)
- USB-C cable (1.5m)
- Quick start guide
- Warranty card

### Initial Setup

1. **Unpack the device** carefully from the box
2. **Connect the power adapter** to the device and plug it into a wall outlet
3. **Press the power button** located on the top right corner
4. **Wait for the LED indicator** to turn solid green (approximately 30 seconds)
5. **Follow the on-screen instructions** to complete setup

## Features

### Smart Mode

Our device features an intelligent Smart Mode that automatically adjusts settings based on your usage patterns. To enable Smart Mode:

1. Navigate to Settings > Advanced
2. Toggle "Smart Mode" to ON
3. The device will learn your preferences over 7 days

### Connectivity

The device supports multiple connectivity options:
- **Wi-Fi**: 802.11ac (2.4GHz and 5GHz)
- **Bluetooth**: 5.0 with extended range
- **Ethernet**: Gigabit (1000 Mbps)

To connect to Wi-Fi:
1. Go to Settings > Network
2. Select your Wi-Fi network
3. Enter the password
4. Wait for connection confirmation

## Troubleshooting

### Device Won't Turn On

If your device doesn't power on:
1. Check that the power adapter is properly connected
2. Try a different power outlet
3. Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds to force restart
4. If the issue persists, contact support

### Connection Issues

If you're experiencing connectivity problems:
1. Restart your router
2. Move the device closer to your router
3. Check for firmware updates in Settings > System > Updates
4. Reset network settings: Settings > Network > Reset

### LED Indicator Meanings

- **Solid Green**: Device is ready
- **Blinking Green**: Device is starting up
- **Solid Red**: Error state
- **Blinking Red**: Critical error, contact support
- **Blue**: Firmware update in progress

## Maintenance

### Regular Cleaning

Clean your device monthly:
1. Power off the device completely
2. Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth
3. Avoid liquid cleaners or abrasive materials
4. Clean ventilation ports with compressed air

### Software Updates

Keep your device updated:
1. Go to Settings > System > Updates
2. Click "Check for Updates"
3. If an update is available, click "Download and Install"
4. The device will restart automatically
