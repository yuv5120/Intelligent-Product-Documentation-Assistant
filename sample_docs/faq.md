# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Q: What is the return policy?

A: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can return the device within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. The device must be in original condition with all accessories and packaging.

### Q: How long does shipping take?

A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. International shipping times vary by location.

### Q: Is the device waterproof?

A: No, the device is not waterproof. It has basic splash resistance (IPX2 rating) but should not be submerged in water or exposed to heavy rain.

## Setup and Installation

### Q: Do I need an account to use the device?

A: Yes, you'll need to create a free account during initial setup. This allows you to access cloud features, receive updates, and sync your settings across devices.

### Q: Can I use the device without internet?

A: Yes, most features work offline. However, some advanced features like cloud sync, voice assistant, and automatic updates require an internet connection.

### Q: How do I reset the device to factory settings?

A: To perform a factory reset:
1. Go to Settings > System > Advanced
2. Scroll down to "Factory Reset"
3. Enter your PIN if prompted
4. Confirm the reset
5. The device will restart and erase all data

**Warning**: Factory reset will delete all your data. Back up important information first.

## Troubleshooting

### Q: Why is my device running slowly?

A: Slow performance can be caused by:
- Too many apps running in the background
- Low storage space (less than 10% free)
- Outdated software
- Cache buildup

Try these solutions:
1. Close unused apps
2. Clear cache: Settings > Storage > Clear Cache
3. Update to the latest software version
4. Restart the device

### Q: The touchscreen is not responsive. What should I do?

A: Try these steps:
1. Clean the screen with a microfiber cloth
2. Remove any screen protector temporarily
3. Restart the device
4. Perform a touchscreen calibration: Settings > Display > Calibrate
5. If the issue persists, contact support

### Q: How do I fix Wi-Fi connection drops?

A: To resolve Wi-Fi connectivity issues:
1. Restart your router and the device
2. Forget the network and reconnect
3. Update router firmware
4. Change Wi-Fi channel on your router (avoid crowded channels)
5. Disable power saving mode: Settings > Battery > Power Saving OFF

## Battery and Power

### Q: How long does the battery last?

A: Battery life depends on usage:
- Light use (reading, browsing): Up to 12 hours
- Medium use (video streaming): 8-10 hours
- Heavy use (gaming, video calls): 5-6 hours

### Q: Can I replace the battery?

A: The battery is not user-replaceable. If you experience battery issues after the warranty period, contact our service center for battery replacement (fee applies).

### Q: Is it safe to leave the device charging overnight?

A: Yes, the device has smart charging technology that prevents overcharging. However, for optimal battery longevity, we recommend unplugging once fully charged.
